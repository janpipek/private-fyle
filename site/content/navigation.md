---
title: "Navigation and selection"
weight: 20
---

Each panel has its own location, cursor, marks, filter, sort order, view and
navigation history. **Tab** switches panels. **Backspace** goes to the parent;
**Alt-Left** and **Alt-Right** move through that panel's history. **Ctrl-L** edits
the active location. **Ctrl-G** opens Locations for drives, places, bookmarks,
frequent folders and configured remotes. **Ctrl-D** adds or removes a bookmark.

In list or icon view, **Space** or **Insert** toggles a mark. **Ctrl-A** selects
all, **∗** inverts marks and **+** selects by glob or substring pattern. **Esc**
clears the current filter first, then marks. Use **Ctrl-U** to swap panel
contents, including their locations and panel state; use **Ctrl-Left** or
**Ctrl-Right** to show the other panel's folder in the active panel.

Press **/** or **Ctrl-F** to filter the current listing as you type. Enter keeps
the filter; Esc clears it. Press **Ctrl-H** to show or hide hidden files.
Breadcrumbs and the location controls in each panel header can also be clicked.

Tree mode is per panel and works with list view. **Ctrl-T** toggles it; in the
tree, Left collapses and Right expands a folder. **Ctrl-1** selects list view and
**Ctrl-2** selects icon view.
